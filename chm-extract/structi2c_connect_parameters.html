Detailed Description

Specify the I2C connect parameters.

Warning
The Bootloader Slave address varies depending on the device (see AN2606).
Note
Not all configurations are supported by STM32 Bootloader, such as address in 7 bits form, analog filter: ENABLE, digital filter: DISABLE.
Recommended I2C parameters : add=0x??, br=400, sm=1, am=0, af=1, df=0, dnf=0, rt=0, ft=0

I2C_Bootloader.
